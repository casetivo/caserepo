A well-capitalized title makes an article, email, presentation, or book look polished. The difficulty is that title case has exceptions: not every word should be capitalized, and style guides do not always agree on every detail.

This guide gives you a practical set of title capitalization rules. When you need a fast result, use the free [Title Case Converter](/title-case) to format a headline before you publish it.

## What is title case?

Title case is a capitalization style used for headings and titles. The first and last word are capitalized, as are most important words in the middle.

For example:

- Sentence case: `How to capitalize a title correctly`
- Title case: `How to Capitalize a Title Correctly`

Title case is commonly used for article headlines, books, films, presentation slides, report headings, and email subject lines.

## The basic title case rules

Use these rules for a reliable general-purpose title:

1. Capitalize the first and last word, whatever they are.
2. Capitalize nouns, pronouns, verbs, adjectives, and adverbs.
3. Usually lowercase short articles: `a`, `an`, and `the`.
4. Usually lowercase short coordinating conjunctions: `and`, `but`, `or`, `nor`, `for`, `so`, and `yet`.
5. Usually lowercase short prepositions such as `at`, `by`, `for`, `in`, `of`, `on`, `to`, and `with` unless they begin or end the title.
6. Capitalize proper nouns, brands, and acronyms exactly as they are normally written.

## Title capitalization examples

| Sentence-style title | Correct title case |
| --- | --- |
| how to write better email subject lines | How to Write Better Email Subject Lines |
| a guide to json formatting | A Guide to JSON Formatting |
| what to do before you publish a blog post | What to Do Before You Publish a Blog Post |
| the best tools for working with csv files | The Best Tools for Working With CSV Files |

Notice that `to`, `a`, `the`, and `with` may remain lowercase in the middle of a title, while words such as `Write`, `Guide`, and `Formatting` are capitalized.

## What about hyphenated words?

For a hyphenated compound in a title, capitalize the main words on both sides of the hyphen when they are important words. For example, `A Well-Designed Landing Page` and `Step-by-Step Writing Tips` are common forms.

Style guides differ on some compounds, so follow your publication's own style guide when one exists. Consistency across your site matters more than a single edge case.

## Title case versus sentence case

Choose title case when you want a traditional, formal headline. Choose [sentence case](/sentence-case) when you want a more conversational style often used in product interfaces, help articles, and modern blogs.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I capitalize "is" in a title?

Yes. `Is` is a verb, and verbs are capitalized in title case.

### Should I capitalize "the" in a title?

Usually not when it appears in the middle of a title. Capitalize it when it is the first or last word.

### Is title case required for blog titles?

No. Both title case and sentence case can work for blogs. Pick the style that matches your brand and use it consistently.
