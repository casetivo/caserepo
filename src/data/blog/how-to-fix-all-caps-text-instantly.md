We have all been there. You spend five minutes typing out a passionate email or a detailed paragraph, only to look up at your screen and realize **YOUR CAPS LOCK KEY WAS ON THE ENTIRE TIME.**

Instead of feeling a surge of panic and reaching for the backspace key, there is a much easier way to fix it.

## Do not retype it!

Retyping large blocks of text is a massive waste of time and increases the likelihood of typos. Furthermore, if you are copying data from an old database or a poorly formatted PDF, you might have hundreds of pages of ALL CAPS text that simply cannot be retyped manually.

## The Solution: Use a Sentence Case Converter

To instantly normalize your text so that it reads like standard, professional English, you need to use a text transformation tool.

1.  **Copy** your messed-up, capitalized text.
2.  **Paste** it into our free online [Sentence Case Converter](/sentence-case).
3.  Click the button.

In milliseconds, the tool will analyze your text, detect where sentences begin and end, and force all the shouting uppercase letters into lowercase—except for the very first letter of each sentence.

## Why Sentence Case?

While you could theoretically convert it to pure lowercase, that would remove the capital letters at the start of your sentences, making it grammatically incorrect. Sentence case is the standard formatting style for almost all online communication.

Save yourself the headache. The next time you make a Caps Lock mistake, just convert it!
